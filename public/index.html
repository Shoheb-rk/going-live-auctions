A FAIR SHOT FOR EVERYONE

The rules of the room.

01 / Raise the bar

English auctions in INR. The first bid meets the starting price; every next bid meets the current price plus the listed increment.

02 / Time to respond

A bid in the final 15 seconds resets the remaining time to 15 seconds. There is no extension limit. The server clock decides when bidding ends.

03 / First accepted wins

All bids commit in a database transaction. Equal bids cannot both win. The first committed bid takes the lead; the other must increase.

04 / Clear outcomes

Listings are immutable. Sellers cannot bid on their own items. Bid history is public. At close, the highest accepted bidder wins at their bid price; no bids means unsold.

Payments, shipping, identity verification, disputes and proxy bidding are outside this academic prototype.
